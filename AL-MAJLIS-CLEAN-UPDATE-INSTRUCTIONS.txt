VERCEL ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES
Open the Al Majlis project in Vercel, then Settings > Environment Variables.
Add these to Production and Preview:

STRIPE_SECRET_KEY = sk_test_...
STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY = pk_test_...
AL_MAJLIS_OWNER_KEY = a private code of at least 20 characters

The two Stripe keys must both be test keys or both be live keys. Never upload
the real values to GitHub. The included .env.example contains placeholders only.

OWNER ACCESS
Local editing at localhost or 127.0.0.1 unlocks every pack automatically.

For the published app, open this once after setting AL_MAJLIS_OWNER_KEY:
https://YOUR-AL-MAJLIS-DOMAIN/?owner=1

Enter your private owner code. That device stays unlocked through a secure,
HttpOnly owner cookie. Do not share the code or the owner link.
