Products and fulfillment

Al Majlis is a digital Islamic and Arabic game night application. Four game modes are free. Optional premium game modes are sold as one-time digital purchases for $1.99 each, or as an all-access premium collection for $7.99. There is no subscription or recurring charge.

Premium access is delivered immediately after Stripe confirms a successful payment. Purchases can be restored on the same browser and device used to complete checkout. Keep the browser's Al Majlis data intact so the verified purchase record remains available.

Refund and cancellation policy

Because premium access is digital and delivered immediately, completed purchases are generally final except where a refund is required by applicable law. We will review requests made within 14 days for duplicate charges, mistaken purchases, or a technical failure to receive the purchased access.

To request help or a refund, email m.alqaddi@outlook.com with the purchase email, purchase date, and the name of the pack. Do not send card numbers. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; the bank's posting time may take 5-10 business days.

All purchases are one-time payments, so there is no subscription to cancel. Nothing in this policy limits rights provided by applicable consumer law.

Privacy policy

Al Majlis stores appearance preferences, sound preferences, and verified Stripe Checkout Session identifiers in the browser so the app can restore access on that device. We do not sell personal information.

Stripe securely processes checkout and card information. Al Majlis receives payment status, the purchased product, and the checkout email when available; it does not receive or store full card details. Vercel hosts the application and its server functions. Optional card-content reports are delivered through FormSubmit, and players are asked not to include private personal details.

Browser data can be removed through browser or device settings, but removing it can also remove same-device purchase restoration. For privacy or support questions, contact the email below.

Terms of use

Purchasing a premium pack grants a personal, non-transferable license to use that content in Al Majlis. The cards, design, audio, and application may not be copied, resold, republished, or redistributed.

Islamic references are provided for learning and gameplay and are not a fatwa or a substitute for qualified scholarly advice. Users should verify sensitive questions with reliable evidence and a qualified scholar. Reported content concerns may be corrected in future releases.

Users must provide accurate payment information and may not attempt to bypass access controls. If a user is not old enough to agree to these terms where they live, a parent or legal guardian must approve the purchase and use.
